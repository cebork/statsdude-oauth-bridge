StatsDude Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

StatsDude Publisher is a private tool used by a single creator, the owner of the StatsDude channels on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, to publish their own videos to their own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no other people can sign up or connect accounts.

What the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the creator's own connected accounts (account id, username, display name), used to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to those accounts, used only when the creator schedules or publishes a video.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) in a private database and are used only to publish the creator's own content and read the status of those publications. The app does not collect data about other users, viewers or followers, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while an account stays connected and are deleted when it is disconnected. Access can also be revoked at any time from the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).
